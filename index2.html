What is Voddic Connect?

A front desk in every guest’s pocket.

Guests shouldn’t have to hunt for a phone number or wait at reception. Voddic Connect links each room to the hotel through a QR code, and gives your staff the tools to answer fast.

📱

Guests scan, not install

A QR card in each room opens the hotel’s services in the phone browser. If the camera isn’t available, guests can type the room number instead.

🚨

Staff respond faster

Emergencies and service requests reach your team’s dashboard right away, with the room number attached and emergency responders flagged.

🏨

Hotels stay in control

Manage rooms, prices, staff, departments and promotions from one place, with your hotel’s name and branding on everything guests see.

Your hotel, one scan away.

Guests also find participating hotels in the Voddic Connect directory, check which rooms are free and call reception directly.

Questions

Quick answers.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the QR card in their room and Voddic Connect opens in the phone’s browser. No account is needed.

What if a guest can’t scan the QR code?

The scan screen has an option to enter the room number instead, and each card also shows a web address guests can open.

How do hotels get their QR codes?

After you add rooms, the QR Room Cards screen generates a card for every room. You can download or print them all at once.

Can my staff respond to emergencies?

Yes. When you add a staff member you can mark them as an emergency responder, and your dashboard shows emergencies as they come in.

I’m a guest. How do I find a hotel?

Use the guest directory to search by hotel name or city, or tap Hotels Near Me.
